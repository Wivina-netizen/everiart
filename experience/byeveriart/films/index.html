ByEveriArt worked with Perfecto Movies to document the celebrations marking the Emir of Gumel’s 45th anniversary on the throne. ByEveriArt organised production logistics, procured what the shoot needed, handled cinematography and completed most of post-production for the documentary. The Gumel Emirate commissioned the project.
