## 1. Privacy notice draft

### Who we are
Everiart operates everiart.com. Contact us about personal information at hello@everiart.com or at the address above.

### What information is handled, and why
- **Contact enquiries:** your name, email address, message and enquiry permission are submitted to Netlify Forms so Everiart can consider and respond to your request. Do not include passwords, card details or confidential documents.
- **Ask Peaches:** messages and recent conversation history are sent through Everiart's server-side AI endpoint to an AI service through Netlify's gateway. Peaches is AI, not a person. Avoid sensitive or confidential material. The website does not create a conversation database; a conversation is held in browser memory while the page is open. This does not mean infrastructure or AI providers keep no records.
- **Email drafts:** choosing Speak with the team opens a draft in your email application. The draft uses excerpts of your enquiry and the direction explored with Peaches. You can edit it and decide whether to send it. Your email provider handles the resulting message.
- **Technical requests:** hosting and video-delivery providers process technical information, such as IP addresses and request details, to deliver and protect the service. Project films are served from Cloudflare R2. Fonts are served from this website.
- **External destinations:** WhatsApp and other external links operate under their own providers' terms and privacy notices when you choose to open them.

### Legal basis and choices — owner/legal review required
Identify and document the lawful basis for responding to project enquiries, processing AI messages, and security logging under applicable Nigerian data-protection law. The contact form asks permission to use the submitted information for the enquiry; it does not subscribe the visitor to marketing. Do not assume one consent checkbox resolves every processing purpose.

### Recipients and transfers — confirm before publication
Confirm the contracting entities and actual settings for Netlify, the AI gateway/provider, Cloudflare and the business email service. Document processing locations, international-transfer safeguards and relevant processor agreements. Do not promise that data stays in Nigeria unless verified.

### Retention — decision needed
Set and implement a retention/deletion schedule for enquiries that do not become projects, project correspondence and accounting records, Netlify form submissions, technical logs and AI-provider data. Confirm backup handling. Do not publish a fixed period before the associated deletion process exists.

### Rights and contact
Visitors can contact hello@everiart.com to ask about access, correction, deletion, objection or other applicable rights concerning their information. Identity verification should be proportionate. Explain any lawful limits when a request cannot be fulfilled. Visitors may also contact the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

### Children and sensitive information — decision needed
Choose the intended audience, how enquiries involving children are handled, and how sensitive personal information sent unexpectedly will be removed or restricted. Peaches must not solicit such details.

### Changes
Publish an effective date only when this notice is approved. Update it when the actual processing changes.

## 2. Cookies and similar technologies draft

The reviewed application contains no analytics tags, advertising pixels or application-set cookies. It does not currently use localStorage to track visitors. Peaches conversation history is held in memory. A legacy contact handoff can read and immediately remove an existing sessionStorage brief; current Peaches handoffs use email drafts instead.

Hosting, abuse-protection or other platform-level behavior must also be checked in a live browser before saying that no provider uses cookies. External sites opened through links may set their own cookies.

**Before publication:** confirm Netlify analytics/injected scripts, runtime cookies, and processor settings. If optional tracking is introduced, implement applicable consent controls before those technologies load, with an equally usable decline option and a way to change the choice. Do not add a cosmetic Accept button when there is nothing optional to choose.

## 3. Website terms draft

This website introduces Everiart's film, design and photography work and lets visitors enquire about projects. Browsing, sending an enquiry or speaking with Peaches does not itself book a project or establish an agreed price, deadline, scope or licence.

Peaches offers ideas to explore. Its suggestions may be incomplete or inaccurate and are not quotes, booking confirmations or contractual commitments. The Everiart team confirms project details in writing.

Project media and other content may belong to Everiart or the relevant rights holders. Viewing the site does not grant permission to reuse, reproduce or commercially exploit that material. Contact the team about permitted use or a rights concern.

Visitors must not misuse the enquiry channels, attempt unauthorized access, distribute malicious code, or submit material they have no right to share. Rate limits may restrict automated abuse.

External links are provided for convenience; their services have separate terms. Contact hello@everiart.com about site errors, questions or complaints.

**Before publication:** have the owner/legal reviewer settle intellectual-property wording, liability provisions, applicable law and dispute handling. Do not insert blanket waivers of consumer or data-protection rights. Paid project contracts should separately define deliverables, approvals, changes, payment, licensing and third-party costs.

## 4. Cancellation and refunds — choices for approval

There is no online checkout on this site. A policy for commissioned services must reflect the actual written project agreement and applicable rights. The following is a proposed structure, not an adopted refund rule:

1. **How to request a change:** email hello@everiart.com with the project name and requested cancellation or rescheduling date.
2. **Before work begins:** decide how advance payments and genuinely incurred third-party costs are handled. Do not describe every deposit as automatically non-refundable.
3. **After work begins:** agree how completed work, committed production costs and unused advance payments are calculated and explained to the client.
4. **Rescheduling:** define notice periods, availability, costs already committed and any agreed rescheduling charge.
5. **If Everiart cannot deliver:** define the offered remedies and treatment of unearned payments, without excluding applicable statutory rights.
6. **Disagreements or defective work:** define the opportunity to resolve concerns, the review contact and escalation route.
7. **Refund timing and method:** choose a realistic processing period and payment method before promising it publicly.
